# Credits and sources

These modules are an homage. They are not built from Tudor's circuits, which are not documented; they model what Tudor and the people who worked with him described.

## On Tudor's work
- DRAM liner notes, *David Tudor: Three Works for Live Electronics* (Lovely Music), with Tudor's own notes on *Untitled*, *Pulsers* and *Phonemes*: https://www.dramonline.org/albums/david-tudor-three-works-for-live-electronics/notes
- Tom Johnson, "David Tudor's Homemade Pulsers", Village Voice, 24 January 1977: https://editions75.com/tvonm/articles/1977/david-tudors-homemade-pulsers.html
- John Driscoll and Matt Rogalsky, "David Tudor's Rainforest: An Evolving Exploration of Resonance", Getty symposium paper: https://getty.edu/research/exhibitions_events/events/david_tudor_symposium/pdf/driscollrogalsky.pdf
- CDM on *Rainforest* (2024): https://cdm.link/2024/07/david-tudor-rainforest/
- EARS realization of *Rainforest IV* (2017): https://www.oliverschneller.net/rainforest
- davidtudor.org, Electronics pages (phase shifters, amplifiers, *Rainforest* diagrams): https://davidtudor.org/Electronics/electronics.html

## DSP
- Topology-preserving state-variable filter: Vadim Zavalishin, *The Art of VA Filter Design*.
- Hilbert all-pass pair coefficients: Olli Niemitalo, "Hilbert transform" (yehar.com).
- Pink noise filter: Paul Kellet.
- Biquad formulas: Robert Bristow-Johnson, *Audio EQ Cookbook*.
- xoshiro128+ generator: David Blackman and Sebastiano Vigna.
